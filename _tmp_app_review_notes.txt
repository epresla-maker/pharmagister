--- MÁSOLD BE EZT (English, ASC "Notes" field) ---

This update (v1.4) introduces the app's first in-app purchase (via RevenueCat,
non-subscription consumable product). Purchasing lets a pharmacy account buy an
additional package of "demand credits", used to post substitution/demand
listings. All core features of the app (registration, browsing substitution
requests, messaging) remain fully free; the IAP only unlocks extra posting
credits and is entirely optional.

In-App Purchase product identifiers:
- pharmagister_4_credits_founder
- pharmagister_4_credits_regular

To test the purchase flow:
1. Sign in with the test account below (role: pharmacy / gyógyszertár).
2. Open the Dashboard and tap "Csomag vásárlása az appban" (Purchase package
   in app).
3. Complete the purchase using the App Store sandbox / StoreKit testing
   environment associated with this build.

If you need a different test account role (substitute pharmacist) or any
other assistance verifying this feature, please let us know.
